# Platform capability matrix

Official documentation was reviewed for the capabilities below. Publication of an endpoint does not confirm Kleva's access, a merchant's eligibility, or permission to automate the proposed sales use case. Require account-specific sandbox evidence and a recorded go/no-go before enabling any channel.

| Channel and phase | Documented capabilities | Approval, restrictions and unknowns | Prerequisites | Operating cost and complexity |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| WhatsApp Business · pilot | Cloud API inbound, reply, media, template send and delivery webhooks | Confirm app review, permissions, WABA registration/subscription, number eligibility, region-specific terms, customer service window and approved templates. AI sales use-case eligibility is a release gate. Do not assume service replies are unmetered. | Meta app, Tech Provider setup as applicable, verified WABA/number, business assets, sandbox and rate card | Provider category/market/billing tier and any BSP markup, plus inference. High.
